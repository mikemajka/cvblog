WindBorne: Powering 24 direct-drive motors is dangerous, especially at their peak power draw (4000 W+). Even with trained RL policies and firmware limits, the system remains challenging to manage safely, which is why an additional layer of purely analog current clamping is implemented. The voltage across a current-sense resistor is compared to a fixed 2.5 V reference. When it exceeds the reference, a monitor IC triggers the FET driver into a skip state that forces four power FETs into linear mode, clamping the current while signalling the MCU to shut the motors down. Because long linear-mode operation stresses the FETs, a complete shutoff is enforced if the condition lasts more than 5 ms, set by an RC timer.
